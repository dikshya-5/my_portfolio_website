About me

With a keen interest in geospatial technologies, environmental applications, and spatial problem-solving, my interests span mapping, surveying, remote sensing, GIS, and spatial data analysis. Through professional internships, fieldwork, and academic research, I have gained practical experience in surveying, drone-based mapping, topographic mapping, and spatial data processing, while exploring the application of remote sensing and deep learning in environmental studies. Alongside my technical work, I enjoy teaching and sharing knowledge, making complex concepts accessible to others. I also have an interest in cartography, graphic design, and visual communication. My involvement in student organisations, volunteering, and collaborative projects has helped me develop leadership, communication, and teamwork skills. I value exchanging ideas, learning from different perspectives, and contributing to collaborative work. With patience, curiosity, and an enthusiasm for continuous learning, I look forward to exploring new technologies and developing meaningful geospatial solutions.
